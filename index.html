Sava 7501 — Hybrid Paddy

Sava 7501 is a premium hybrid paddy seed by Savannah Seeds, engineered for superior yield, disease resistance, and adaptability across diverse agro-climatic conditions. Ideal for farmers seeking consistent, high-quality harvests.

Read more...
  • High yield potential with excellent grain quality
  • Strong resistance to major diseases
  • Adaptable across diverse agro-climatic zones
  • Medium-duration variety (120–130 days)
  • Net Weight: 3 Kg per pack
  • Certified F1 Hybrid Rice Seed
  • Treated with Thiram fungicide for seed protection
  • Germination rate: >90%

Prepare a well-puddled nursery bed. Sow seeds at 15–20 kg/ha rate. Transplant 20–25 day old seedlings at 20×15 cm spacing. Maintain proper water management and apply recommended fertilizers for best results.

Want to know more?

Yes, Sava 7501 is a completely safe and certified product. All seeds are tested and approved under stringent quality standards.

Sava 7501 is best planted during the Kharif season (June–July). It performs well in both irrigated and rain-fed conditions.
